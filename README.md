## 🧠 Habits & Workflow

### 🎯 Focus blocks
- Work in **long, uninterrupted blocks** with a macOS Focus mode on: notifications off, one task at a time.
- Pick the task in **Things** *before* starting the block, so there's nothing to decide in the middle.

### 🧍 Active breaks
- **Alternate sitting and standing** with the crank desk — the crank itself is a mini break.
- **Swap keyboards** (AK820 ↔ K860) and pointing devices (trackball ↔ trackpad) to vary posture.
- Rest your eyes regularly: look away from the screen at something far away.

### 🗂️ Second brain
- Everything goes into **Obsidian**: ideas, technical decisions, snippets, meeting notes.
- Link notes to each other instead of filing them in deep folders.
- If I solve something twice, it becomes a note.
